Privacy Policy
Gill Jam: Fish Escape Puzzle · Android · com.rogasoft.gilljam
Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Gill Jam (the “Game”), why, and what choices you have. The Game has no user accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail address or other contact details.

1Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Rogasoft, Warsaw, Poland (“we”, “us”).
Contact for all privacy matters: przemek5504@gmail.com.

2Data stored only on your device

The Game saves your progress (current level, coins, collected fish, pond decorations, daily-reward streak, settings and the state of an unfinished level) locally on your device. We do not receive this data. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

3Data processed by service providers

The Game uses the following services from Google. They may collect information from your device as described below.

Google AdMob – advertising

  • Data: advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and interactions with ads.
  • Purpose: showing ads (including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch), limiting ad frequency, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud and – only with your consent – personalising ads.
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Analytics for Firebase – game analytics

  • Data: app instance ID, in-game events (e.g. level started/finished, boosters used, rewarded-ad offers), device information, approximate location (country/region derived from IP address).
  • Purpose: understanding how the Game is played so we can balance difficulty and improve it.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage is off by default and is only enabled if you give consent in the privacy message.

Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports

  • Data: crash and error details (stack trace), device model, OS version, app version, a Crashlytics installation identifier, and technical context such as the current level and screen.
  • Purpose: finding and fixing bugs.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Send crash reports.

Google Play – in-app purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The Game only receives information needed to deliver your purchase (product, order ID, purchase token and status). See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

🐟We do not sell your personal data. Google’s practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5Your privacy choices

  • Consent message: if you are in a region where it is required, the Game shows a privacy message (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch. You can accept, refuse or choose per purpose.
  • Changing your choice: open Settings → Privacy options at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Crash reports: Settings → Send crash reports.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).

6How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for 2 months (our Google Analytics retention setting); data associated with the advertising ID is kept for a shorter period set by Google.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and related identifiers are kept for 90 days.
  • AdMob and Google Play: according to Google’s own retention policies.

7International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Google LLC is certified) and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

8Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to przemek5504@gmail.com.

Because the Game has no accounts, we usually cannot link data held by Google to you. If you contact us, it helps to include the approximate date you played and your device model. You can also use Google’s privacy tools.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland it is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl). You can also contact the authority in your own country.

9Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all locally stored data, including your progress.
  • To ask us to delete data held by our service providers, e-mail przemek5504@gmail.com. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.

10Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it. In some countries, consent to personalised ads requires a parent’s permission below a higher age (up to 16 in the EU).

11Security

Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Local game data is protected by Android’s app sandbox.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The current version is always available at this address and in the Game (Settings → Privacy policy). The date of the last update is shown at the top.
